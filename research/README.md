# research

Evidence behind cmdfound's decisions, not instructions.

- `SUMMARY.md` - how much of Linux the channels reach, and which sources are
  worth a channel.
- `decisions.md` - the owner's rulings that shape cmdfound.
- `bin/` - the scripts that rebuild every number: `./build.sh [SOURCE]` writes
  `sets/` and downloads to `dl/`, neither of them committed.
